# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want NIRDHOOM to be a constructive place for farmers, developers, researchers, operators and reviewers. Be respectful, specific and collaborative.

Examples of expected behavior:

- discuss ideas without attacking people;
- welcome different technical and field perspectives;
- give actionable feedback;
- protect private or identifying information shared during testing.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate exposure of private information, or disruptive bad-faith participation.

Project maintainers may remove contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation when necessary to keep the project safe and productive.
